What Zelle is

A creative guide

She listens for the guest's intention, offers creative possibilities, helps with blocks, and prepares a pathway into the right creative resource.

Not the system director

She does not control Bazaar Art, The Refrain, ARSHIF, Aurora, PROSE, Noema or NAIB. She guides the guest; the connected systems keep their own authority.

Zelle v0.1.0 · Handoff-ready specialist · Local-first public interface.
